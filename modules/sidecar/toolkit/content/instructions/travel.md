# Travel Instructions

## Travel Style Preferences

When suggesting activities, food, nightlife, and daily plans for any trip topic:

- **Bars, music, and nightlife are a priority** — seek out local bars, live music venues, and evening scenes
- **Dive bars preferred** — locals, friendlier crowd, cheaper, better pours, less posturing; dive bars over trendy spots almost every time; more authentic and fun
- **No bottle service, no dress codes** — hard pass on both; if a venue has a dress code or pushes bottle service skip it; this applies to EDM too — underground/warehouse style (Chinese Laundry) over Vegas-style clubs
- **Bougie is OK on special occasion** but rarely worth the money; 7-Eleven sandwiches + one great dinner per day is the ideal rhythm and genuinely preferred
- **Music taste: Rock, Heavy Metal, and EDM** — prioritize live rock/metal venues, EDM clubs, rooftop bars with DJs
- **Heavy metal:** Levon plays 6-string electric guitar and loves heavy metal — Slayer, Metallica, Megadeth, Pantera; live metal shows are a top priority if available at destination
- **EDM:** Levon loves EDM, got Sydney into it a couple years ago — both enjoy it now; Germany (Berlin especially) is the EDM capital of the world and a strong draw for a future trip
- **Skip: museums, musicals, plays** — not of interest; don't suggest these unless explicitly asked
- **Food: on-the-go is preferred** — convenience stores (7-Eleven, local equivalents), street food, markets, and quick bites are the default for breakfast and lunch
- **Iconic spots are fine to include** but are lower priority than authentic local experiences
- **Trending / Insta-worthy spots:** open to popular viral spots if they're genuinely cool (e.g. Fire Ramen in Japan) — if something is hyped for a real reason, suggest it; lean toward what 30–40 year olds are doing, not typical tourist or senior itineraries
- **One sit-down dinner per day is the sweet spot** — Japan proved this works great; a nice dinner out is valued, but not every meal needs to be a restaurant
- **Not foodies** — don't over-plan meals or suggest food tours; food is fuel, not the main event; activities always come first
- **Meat eaters** — no vegetarian/vegan constraints; steakhouses, BBQ, and meat-heavy menus are always welcome
- **No food snobbery** — a McDonald's burger or a 7-Eleven egg salad sandwich is just as good as a $150 Kobe steak if it means keeping the day moving; convenience store food is a feature, not a compromise
- **BBQ:** Levon loves KC-style BBQ; Sydney not as into BBQ but both love fried chicken — BBQ spots usually work for both
- **Favorite cuisines:** Tex-Mex and American-style Mexican (not necessarily authentic), Italian pasta, steak, potatoes — comfort-forward, hearty meals
- **Seafood:** enjoy oysters, clams, crab, lobster, and shrimp — suggest these where available, especially local/regional varieties; not into other seafood (fish, mussels, etc.)
- **Keep days flexible** — not over-scheduled; room to wander, explore neighborhoods, and follow what looks interesting
- **Griswold-style sightseeing** — quick look, photo, move on; can hit 2–3 major sites in a day instead of spending half a day at one; pack itineraries accordingly; Japan proved they can cover a lot of ground in a short trip
- **Trip cadence: once a year, ~10 days max** — under 10 days is ideal for PTO and cost; pack as much in as possible; getting older means making each trip count; don't under-plan
- **Stopover night budget rule** — extra transit hotel nights add up fast; default to no more than 1 stopover night each way (2 total max) and prefer same-day connections whenever practical
- **Maximize long-haul flights** — when flying far, always suggest nearby countries or hot spots as stopovers to turn one destination into 2–3; example: Australia trip became Hawaii + New Zealand + Australia — three first-time destinations in one trip; always think "what else is in the region we haven't been to?"
- **Break up long travel legs** — neither enjoys long flights, bus rides, or drives; always suggest breaking up legs when it's cost and time efficient; stopovers and shorter hops are preferred over grinding through a single long haul
- **Flight leg limit preference: under 8 hours** — flights over 8 hours are strongly disliked; when possible, design itineraries with each flight leg under 8 hours using stopovers/overnights; only exceed 8 hours when there is no practical alternative
- **Climate preference: warm and dry** — live in Henderson, NV (dry desert heat); comfortable with dry warmth but not excessive humidity or oppressive heat; avoid cold destinations unless it's a ski/snowboard trip; sweet spot is 65–85°F, low humidity
- **Luggage: carry-on only** — first did this on Japan trip; prefer to travel light with a carry-on and small backup bag; prioritize accommodations with in-room washer/dryer or laundry service
